# 《再来一次，我就洗澡》MiniMax H3 视频提示词

> 来源：`07_final_screenplay.md`
> 
> 模式建议：I2VA 或 L2VA。先用资产图固定团团、回溯器和客厅，再按镜头生成；没有参考图时可退回 T2VA。
> 
> 成片：9:16，24fps，约 3 分钟。每个 Shot 建议 4-8 秒，回溯重复由剪辑完成。

## subject_definitions

```text
<Subject 1> TuanTuan, a small round orange-and-white domestic shorthair cat. The left ear has a distinctive dark orange patch. The cat has a white muzzle and chest, amber-green eyes, a soft green collar with a tiny metal tag, and consistent compact proportions. TuanTuan is expressive but never aggressive or injured.

<Subject 2> Lin, a calm young Chinese man shown mainly through hands, forearms, partial torso and occasional half-body framing. He wears light gray soft home clothes and uses slow, gentle, non-threatening gestures. His voice is heard off screen more often than his face is shown.

<Object 1> A palm-sized rounded black rewind device with a single circular button and one status light. Blue means ready, yellow means warning, red means quiet. It stays beside the low cabinet.

<Object 2> A pale-yellow dry bath towel.

<Object 3> A warm cream-colored thick towel spread in a patch of afternoon sunlight.

<Object 4> Small golden-brown dried fish snacks.

<Object 5> A low circular black robot vacuum cleaner.
```

## scene_definition

```text
One ordinary Chinese apartment living room on a weekend afternoon. Pale cream walls, light oak floor, fabric sofa on the left, short cabinet near the sofa, storage cabinet with a narrow door gap, large window casting warm rectangular sunlight onto the floor, rewind device beside the low cabinet. Keep the furniture layout, window direction, sunlight direction and prop positions consistent throughout. Warm domestic comedy, realistic household texture, gentle soft shadows.
```

## summary

```text
<Subject 1> wants to avoid being cleaned and discovers that <Object 1> rewinds the room three seconds whenever its button is pressed. It repeats the escape and steals infinite dried fish. <Subject 2> learns the rule, stops chasing, and offers a warm towel instead. <Subject 1> chooses gentle cleaning and finally chooses a dried fish over rewinding. The ending is affectionate and comedic, not punitive.
```

## retention_analysis

```text
Keep <Subject 1>'s orange-and-white fur, dark left-ear patch and green collar in every shot. Keep <Object 1>'s single button, position beside the low cabinet, and color logic: blue ready, yellow warning, red quiet. Keep the sofa, storage cabinet, low cabinet, window and sunlight direction fixed. Keep <Subject 2> mostly partial-body to reduce identity drift. Do not show forced bathing, injury, abuse, panic, extra cats, extra people or new locations.
```

```text

[Shot 2 | 0:06-0:12] <Subject 1> jumps onto the fabric sofa and curls into a compact innocent ball, pretending nothing happened. The same room layout and sunlight remain unchanged. Use a gentle sofa-height follow and stop when the cat is still.

[Shot 4 | 0:20-0:25] Match the exact framing of Shot 3. Show <Subject 1>, <Object 2> and the tiny fish crumbs moving backward three seconds to their previous positions. The blue light pulses once. Use a clean reverse-motion effect, no morphing and no new objects.

[Shot 5 | 0:25-0:32] In the same framing, <Subject 2> reaches with <Object 2>. <Subject 1> deliberately raises one paw and presses the single button on <Object 1>. The blue light turns on. Hold for a small comedic pause.

[Shot 6 | 0:32-0:38] Repeat the exact Shot 5 composition and reverse the action three seconds. The towel, paw and hand return to their earlier positions. Add one short electronic beep and a soft reverse-air sound.

[Shot 7 | 0:38-0:52] Use three short match-cut beats in the same living room: <Object 2> enters and <Subject 1> presses the button; then <Object 5> turns toward the cat and <Subject 1> presses the button again. Each reset has one blue flash. Keep the physical actions simple and readable.

[Shot 8 | 0:52-0:58] <Subject 1> lies on its back on the floor, all paws relaxed, tail flicking once like a tiny victory announcement. <Subject 2> stops in the background. Use a low wide shot and a warm comic pause.

[Shot 9 | 0:58-1:05] <Subject 2> places one <Object 4> beside <Object 1> and slowly retreats. <Subject 1> watches from the sofa edge. Medium shot, clear spatial relationship, quiet anticipation.

[Shot 10 | 1:05-1:12] <Subject 1> gently takes <Object 4>, looks back at <Subject 2>, then presses <Object 1> with one paw. The blue light pulses. Use a low three-quarter angle and deliberate timing.

[Shot 11 | 1:12-1:20] Match the previous composition. The room rewinds three seconds: <Object 4> returns beside <Object 1> while the fish remains in <Subject 1>'s mouth. The cat's eyes widen with delighted realization.

[Shot 12 | 1:20-1:31] Show a readable comedic montage: <Subject 1> takes the same fish, presses the button, rewinds, and retains the fish. Each loop adds another visible fish around the cheeks while the floor fish returns to the same spot. Keep the cat identity and camera axis stable.

[Shot 13 | 1:31-1:38] Close on <Subject 1> guarding the growing mouthful of fish with both paws, back turned toward <Subject 2>. The owner counts from off screen. The cat looks proud and guilty. Do not generate subtitles.

[Shot 14 | 1:38-1:46] <Subject 2> crouches beside the low cabinet and places one palm over the edge of <Object 1>. <Subject 1> approaches from the same position. Use a low two-subject shot; the gesture is firm but gentle, never forceful.

[Shot 15 | 1:46-1:53] After the three-second rewind, <Subject 2>'s hand remains pressed on <Object 1> while the room returns to the earlier state. <Subject 1> approaches again. Exact composition, one blue pulse.

[Shot 16 | 1:53-2:00] On the second and third loops, <Subject 2> withdraws his hand early. <Subject 1>'s paw stops in midair before touching <Object 1>. The owner calmly says that the cat is not running fast but restarting fast. Alternate a paw close-up and a partial hand close-up.

[Shot 17 | 2:00-2:08] <Subject 2> sets <Object 2> aside and spreads <Object 3> in the patch of sunlight, then sits far away with hands on knees. Wide vertical composition, inviting golden light, clear distance and patience.

[Shot 18 | 2:08-2:16] <Subject 1> looks from <Object 3> to <Object 1>, touches the button and retracts the paw. It tries again. The status light changes from blue to yellow and the device makes a low warning tone. Close-up on paw and light, suspenseful but gentle.

[Shot 19 | 2:16-2:24] <Subject 2> pushes <Object 2> farther away and stays still. <Subject 1> watches, then walks to <Object 3>, placing one front paw first and lowering its body. Use a side-on medium shot and warm sunlight.
```

## overall_soundscape

```text
Quiet weekend living-room ambience, soft distant city noise through the window, faint fabric and paw movement, subtle robot-vacuum motor during Shot 7, gentle towel friction during Shots 17-21. Each button press produces one short clean electronic beep. Each rewind adds a very light reverse-air texture, never an explosion. Keep dialogue intelligible and close when heard off screen.
```


## H3 生成注意事项

- 需要参考图时使用 `I2VA` 或 `L2VA`，并保持 `<Subject 1>`、`<Object 1>`、`ENV_001` 的引用一致。
- 回溯使用相同构图的两次生成片段加后期倒放/跳剪，不要求单次生成真实时间倒流。
- 每个 Shot 只保留一个主要动作；复杂蒙太奇拆为多个短片段。
- 生成失败时优先降低动作数量、缩短时长、固定机位，再重试。
